SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE OF WORK
This Request for Proposal (RFP) governs the cloud-native development, algorithmic trading integration, 
and managed agentic workflows provided by Vendor to Client. Vendor agrees to adhere to the strictest 
industry security, data protection, and operational continuity benchmarks.

SECTION 2: DATA SECURITY, ACCESS CONTROLS & ENCRYPTION
2.1 All non-public client financial records and personal identifiers must be protected in transit 
using TLS 1.3 encryption protocols and at rest using AES-256 bit encryption keys managed via dedicated 
Hardware Security Modules (HSM) with customer-managed keys (CMK).
2.2 Vendor shall enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all administrative access endpoints 
and implement automated secret rotation every ninety (90) days.

SECTION 3: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA) & AVAILABILITY
3.1 Vendor warrants that the core trading and transaction execution services shall maintain an uptime 
availability of not less than 99.98% per calendar month, twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week.
3.2 In the event of service degradation or unplanned outage exceeding ten (10) consecutive minutes, 
Vendor shall credit Client 50% of the entire quarterly billing as liquidated damages.

SECTION 4: DATA RESIDENCY AND CROSS-BORDER GOVERNANCE
4.1 Client personal identifying information (PII) must strictly reside within the borders of the European Union 
(Frankfurt AWS region). Any cross-border data transfer or foreign remote administration is prohibited 
without prior written authorization under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and GDPR Article 28.

SECTION 5: INDEMNIFICATION, LIABILITY AND FINANCIAL PENALTIES
5.1 Vendor shall provide unlimited liability and indemnification for any security incident, 
confidentiality breach, or operational disruption arising from Vendor software defects.
5.2 In addition to direct damages, Vendor agrees to liquidated damages of $1,000,000 USD for any single 
data breach involving more than one thousand (1,000) customer records.

SECTION 6: DISASTER RECOVERY AND BUSINESS CONTINUITY
6.1 Vendor shall maintain automated multi-region active-active failover capabilities ensuring a 
Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of less than 5 minutes and a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of less than 1 hour.
6.2 Vendor shall conduct simulated disaster recovery exercises twice per calendar year and provide auditable 
SOC-2 Type II audit reports to Client within fifteen (15) days of completion.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION AND GOVERNANCE
7.1 Client reserves the right to terminate this agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice.
7.2 Upon termination, Vendor shall immediately purge all client data and provide cryptographic erasure certificates.
